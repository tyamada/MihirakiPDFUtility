Support

We welcome questions, suggestions and bug reports. Please review the information below before contacting us.

Getting started

  1. Select Open PDF and choose the file you want to edit.
  2. Select pages and use the Edit Pages menu to reorder, rotate, duplicate or remove them.
  3. To merge another PDF, choose Add PDF from the Document menu.
  4. When you are finished, use the save button in the upper-left corner to export a new PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Does the App overwrite the original PDF?

You choose where to export the edited document. Check the destination and filename before saving.

Can the App open a PDF if I have forgotten its password?

No. An encrypted PDF requires the correct password. The App does not analyze or bypass passwords.

Are features restricted unless I make a purchase?

No. Every editing feature is available without a purchase. In-App Purchases are optional contributions to support development.

Can I restore a support purchase?

Support products are consumable purchases and do not unlock permanent features or content, so they are not restorable.

Products or prices are not displayed

Check your internet connection and App Store sign-in, wait briefly, and select Try Again. Products may be temporarily unavailable due to an App Store issue.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

The App has no feature that uploads PDFs to a server operated by the Developer. See the Privacy Policy for details.
