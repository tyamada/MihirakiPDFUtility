Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how tyamada (the “Developer”) handles information in Mihiraki PDF Utility (the “App”).

1. PDF files

PDF files selected in the App and the edits you make are processed on your device to provide the App’s features. The App has no feature that sends, stores or collects your PDF files on a server operated by the Developer.

2. Data collection

The App does not send personal data such as your name, email address, location, contacts, browsing history or advertising identifier to a server operated by the Developer. No user account is required.

3. On-device diagnostic logs

The App stores limited diagnostic records on the device to help identify operation sequences, unexpected termination, slowdowns and other malfunctions. Records can include event types, outcomes, processing time and page counts. They do not include file names, file paths, PDF contents, document metadata, passwords, the device name or other personal information.

Diagnostic logs and performance test results can be viewed and copied by the user. The App has no feature that sends them externally. Log records older than 14 days are deleted automatically.

4. In-App Purchases

Optional support purchases are processed through Apple’s StoreKit and the App Store. The Developer does not directly receive or store payment details such as credit card numbers. Apple’s privacy policy applies to information processed by Apple.

5. Third-party services

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs intended for behavioral tracking. Apple’s terms and policies apply to the App Store, StoreKit and operating-system services.

6. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub’s Privacy Statement may apply to technical information generated when you access the site. This website does not use its own cookies, advertising or analytics.

7. Children’s privacy

The App does not intentionally collect personal data from people of any age.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s features or applicable requirements change. Material changes will be announced on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the Developer through GitHub Issues.
