“Mihiraki PDF Utility” is a versatile tool designed to organize, edit, and read PDF pages on your iPhone and iPad. Ideal for study, work documents, and reading digitized books and manga.

[Key Features]
■ Spread (Mihiraki) View
View two pages side by side with full support for Right-to-Left (RTL) reading order, perfect for manga and books.

■ Advanced Page Splitting
Split two-page spreads into separate individual pages. Supports both vertical (left/right) and horizontal (top/bottom) split.

■ Flexible Page Reordering & Merging
Rearrange pages intuitively with drag-and-drop. Easily combine multiple PDF files, insert blank pages, and rotate or delete pages.

■ Margin Cropping
Trim unwanted scanner margins and borders across selected pages with customizable margins.

■ Image Folder Import & PDF Creation
Import image folders (scans or photos) directly into the app, adjust page order, and generate a brand-new PDF document.

■ Official Sample PDFs
Download official sample manga and documents directly within the app to try spread viewing and editing features right away.

■ Security & Metadata
Open and export password-protected PDFs. Edit document properties including title, author, subject, and keywords.

■ On-Device Diagnostics & Audit Logs
Run performance tests and view local diagnostic logs automatically retained on your device for 14 days. If an issue occurs, you can review the diagnostic test report before choosing whether to share it manually. Nothing is sent automatically, and personal data or PDF contents are never included.

■ Designed for iPad & Large Displays
Supports hardware keyboard shortcuts, trackpad navigation, and multitasking features like Split View and Slide Over to take full advantage of iPad’s larger screen.

■ Full-Screen Preview & Zoom
Inspect fine details with high-resolution rendering, pinch-to-zoom (1x–5x), and double-tap zoom.

■ Multilingual Support
Available in 14 languages, including English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, German, French, Spanish, Italian, and more.

■ 100% On-Device Processing
All PDF processing takes place entirely on your device. Your documents are never uploaded to any external server.

■ Optional Supporter Icons
All PDF editing and viewing features are completely free. Optional one-time supporter tips are available to unlock special Bronze, Silver, and Gold app icons if you wish to support development.
